[ FEATURES ]

Built for thinking in 3D

01
True 3D
Cells fill a volumetric grid — not a board wrapped on a sphere. Mines can be anywhere in the volume: above, below, behind.
02
Face-Adjacent Only
Numbers count mines in cells that share a face — never the edges or corners. For cubes that means 6 neighbors instead of 26. A deliberate departure from classic Minesweeper that makes the game enoyable in 3D (and takes a couple boards to internalize).
03
20+ Cell Shapes
Cubes, octahedra, dodecahedra, icosahedra, and more. Each shape changes how cells connect — and how mines hide. Mobile: 5–10 shapes; full set on Steam & Quest.
04
10 Themes
Ten visual themes, including a color-blind palette. Pick what feels right for the run.
05
Free on Mobile, Forever
No ads. No in-app purchases. Free on iOS and Android for life. The Steam & Quest builds are paid — that's where larger grids and the full shape set live.
[ SHAPES ]

Change the shape, change the puzzle

Swap the cube for a hexagonal prism and every number on the board means something new. Each shape carries its own face count, its own neighbors, its own rhythm. Full catalog on Steam & Quest; mobile ships with a curated set.
